How it works

Three steps to a calmer household

1
You set the tasks and rewards

Create tasks with star values: one-off or recurring on specific days. Then build a reward market with things your kids actually care about like; extra screen time, movie night pick, choose dinner. You control what's available and when.

2
Kids complete tasks and earn stars

Your child ticks off their tasks, optionally submits a photo as proof, and sends it for your approval. Then, you get a notification. Stars land in their balance the moment you tap approve.

3
Stars get spent in the family market

Kids browse the reward market and claim what they've saved up for. They feel in control of their own balance. You stay in control of what they can earn. Nobody argues about screen time anymore.

Questions

Things parents usually ask

What age is Starney for?
It works for anyone. The child view is simple enough for a 5-year-old and purposeful enough for teenagers. You calibrate task complexity and star values to match your child's age and ability.
Can two parents manage the same family?
Yes, on Premium. Multiple parents can join the same family space with equal access. Both can create tasks, approve completions, and manage the reward market. Everything stays in sync in real time.
How do children join the family?
You share a 6-character invite code from your parent dashboard. Your child creates their account, enters the code, and appears on your dashboard instantly. The code refreshes regularly for security.
What happens if a child doesn't complete a task?
Nothing automatic. Starney doesn't punish, it rather motivates positively. Incomplete tasks stay on the list. We deliberately left out penalties because positive reinforcement builds better long-term habits in children.
Can I limit when rewards are available?
Yes. "Movie night pick" only on Fridays and Saturdays. "Stay up 30 minutes later" only on weekends. You choose which days each reward is available. Kids see them greyed out on off-days.
Is my family's data private?
Completely. Your family data, including proof photos, is private to your family only. We don't sell data, we don't run ads, and children's data is handled in compliance with child privacy laws. Read our privacy policy for more details.
